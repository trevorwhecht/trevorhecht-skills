## UX Laws

Design checks for a new screen. Where a UB rule already sets the floor, it is cited and not repeated.

- **Fitts:** frequent actions get bigger hit areas placed closer to where the user already is (UB-15 is the floor).
- **Hick:** fewer choices per screen decide faster (UB-24).
- **Miller:** group lists and forms in chunks of about 7 or fewer.
- **Doherty:** respond in under 400 ms or show progress (UB-47).
- **Postel:** accept messy input such as phone formats, spaces and case, and store it clean.
- **Jakob:** work like the sites people already know, for example the logo at top left goes home.
- **Frequency of use:** the most used action is the nearest and largest.
